# Privacy Policy for FaceADE

The faceADE iOS app does not collect any user data. Any recordings you make through the app stay on your device. faceADE uses the Apple ARKit framework to access the TrueDepth camera and analyze facial geometry and movement in real time. The app processes facial-landmark data and calculated face metrics while it is open. All processing occurs locally on the device; the app does not transmit images, depth maps, or identifiable facial data.

Users may optionally generate CSV files or save screenshots or recordings of their sessions. These files are stored only on the user’s device and may be exported solely at the user’s discretion. No face data or recordings are shared with third parties, and the app retains no facial data once the session ends.
